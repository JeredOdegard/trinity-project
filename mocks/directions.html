Trinity Project · direction mocks

Grace coffee, Trinity bag campaign. Same three screens in three visual directions. Pick A, B, or C (or mix parts). Beans animate on screen 3.

Flow assumed in every mock
  1. Each sticker has its own QR: grace.coffee/t/x7k2m9 (unguessable code, one per bag).
  2. First scan of a code: write a message. Submit plants a leaf and matches you with the previous submitter (first ever gets a seed message from Grace).
  3. Every later scan of the same code skips the form and shows your match, your leaf, and progress to the cocoa rain.
  4. Whole tree: all leaves, a few random messages surfaced as bubbles, tap any leaf to read. Past the threshold, cocoa beans rain.

A Garden Journal

Cream paper, serif type, botanical line tree. Quiet, devotional, reads like a handwritten note tucked in the bag.

B Night Grove

Espresso dark, leaves glow like lanterns, cocoa beans fall like embers. Most dramatic payoff when the rain starts.

C Kraft Sticker

Matches the physical bag: kraft paper, black ink, stamp type, numbered bags. Feels like a continuation of the packaging.
